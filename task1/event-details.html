Tech Fest 2026

A celebration of inovation , techonolgy and creativity

About the Event

Techfest is India’s premier international science and technology festival, annually hosted by the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay. Established to provide a platform for student community to develop and showcase their technical prowess, it has grown into Asia's largest festival of its kind. The event attracts over 175,000 attendees from across the globe, featuring a diverse lineup of high-octane robotics competitions, cutting-edge tech exhibitions, and hands-on workshops. Renowned scientists, industry leaders, and innovators deliver inspiring keynote lectures. Ultimately, Techfest serves as a vital hub for fostering scientific temper, global networking, and groundbreaking technological innovation.
