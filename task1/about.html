About Campus Events

Take the next step toward your future at the Spring Career & Internship Expo on Thursday, October 22, from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the Student Center Ballroom. This event connects you directly with top employers looking to hire for internships and full-time positions. Recruiters from over 50 companies will be on-site, eager to meet students from all majors. Dress in professional attire, bring multiple copies of your resume, and get ready to network. Your career starts here!
